
       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. ADDTWO.

       DATA DIVISION.
       WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
       01 NUM1       PIC 9(5).
       01 NUM2       PIC 9(5).
       01 RESULT     PIC 9(6).

       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
           DISPLAY "ENTER FIRST NUMBER: "
           ACCEPT NUM1

           DISPLAY "ENTER SECOND NUMBER: "
           ACCEPT NUM2

           COMPUTE RESULT = NUM1 + NUM2

           DISPLAY "FIRST NUMBER: " NUM1
           DISPLAY "SECOND NUMBER: " NUM2
           DISPLAY "SUM IS: " RESULT

           STOP RUN.
